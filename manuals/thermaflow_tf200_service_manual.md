# ThermaFlow TF-200 Commercial Tankless Water Heater — Service Manual (sample)

This is a fictional sample manual included to demonstrate the troubleshooting assistant.

# 1 Safety

DANGER: If you smell gas, do not operate any electrical switches. Close the manual gas shut-off valve, evacuate the area and contact the gas supplier from a safe location. Do not resume service until the leak has been located and repaired.

WARNING: Disconnect 120 VAC power at the service disconnect before removing the front cover. The igniter produces high voltage during the ignition sequence.

WARNING: Water inside the heat exchanger may exceed 180°F (82°C). Allow the unit to cool for 15 minutes and relieve pressure before opening any water connection.

# 2 Error Codes

## E1 — Ignition Failure

The unit attempted three ignition trials without detecting a flame. Common causes are: the manual gas valve is closed, air in a new gas line, inlet gas pressure below 4.0 in. W.C. (natural gas) or 8.0 in. W.C. (propane), a fouled or cracked igniter, or an incorrect igniter gap.

Procedure: 1) Confirm the manual gas valve is fully open. 2) Measure static and dynamic inlet gas pressure at the inlet test port with all appliances firing; natural gas must stay between 4.0 and 10.5 in. W.C. 3) Inspect the igniter: the electrode gap must be 0.12–0.16 in. (3–4 mm). Replace the igniter (part TF-IGN-01) if the ceramic is cracked or the electrode is eroded. 4) Reset the unit by pressing ON/OFF twice.

## E2 — Flame Loss During Operation

Flame was established but lost more than five times in one call for heat. Check the flame rod for oxidation and clean it with fine steel wool. Flame rod current should read at least 1.0 µA DC. Also inspect the condensate trap: a blocked trap can back up and extinguish the flame. Verify the venting is not obstructed.

## E3 — Overheat / High-Limit Trip

The outlet temperature exceeded 200°F (93°C) or the high-limit switch opened. The most common cause is scale buildup in the heat exchanger, which reduces flow. Descale the heat exchanger using the isolation valves and a circulating pump with an approved descaling solution for 45 minutes. Also check that the outlet thermistor reads 10 kΩ at 77°F (25°C). The high-limit switch is a manual reset device; press the red button on the switch after the cause is corrected.

## E5 — Fan / Venting Fault

The combustion fan speed did not reach target or the air pressure switch did not close. Check the vent termination for bird nests, snow or ice. Maximum vent length is 60 equivalent feet of 3 in. PVC; each 90° elbow counts as 5 feet. Confirm the pressure switch hose is not cracked or full of condensate. Measure fan supply voltage at connector J7: 120 VAC with the fan commanded on. Replace the fan assembly (TF-FAN-02) if voltage is present but the fan does not turn.

# 3 No Hot Water / Low Flow

The unit requires a minimum flow of 0.5 GPM to activate. If no error code is shown and the unit does not fire, clean the inlet water filter screen located behind the cold water inlet connection. Check for a crossover in the plumbing (hot and cold lines connected together), which can prevent the flow sensor from detecting demand. Fluctuating temperature at low flow is normal below 0.6 GPM.

# 4 Annual Maintenance

Clean the inlet filter, flush the heat exchanger in hard-water areas (above 7 grains per gallon), inspect the venting and condensate line, and test the pressure relief valve by lifting the lever briefly.
